Local Healthcare in Paris

Pharmacies & Emergency Guidance for Paris

Key healthcare resources, 24/7 pharmacies, and emergency services in Paris.

💊 24-Hour Pharmacies in Paris

For urgent night and weekend medications, Pharmacie des Champs-Élysées (84 Ave des Champs-Élysées) is open 24/7/365. Pharmacie de la Porte Maillot (68 Ave de la Grande Armée) also provides continuous service. Look for the illuminated green cross.

🚑 Paris Public Emergency Care

For acute life-threatening emergencies, call 15 (SAMU) or European emergency 112. Major public hospital emergency departments (*Urgences*) include Hôpital Cochin and Hôpital Saint-Louis. For doctor home calls in Paris, contact SOS Médecins at 36 24.

Questions about Paris

Frequently asked questions

Can I consult an English-speaking doctor while in Paris?

Yes. Your Doctor Abroad connects tourists, students, and expats in Paris with fluent English-speaking physicians online for rapid consultations and advice.

Where can I find 24-hour pharmacies in Paris for urgent prescriptions?

Pharmacie des Champs-Élysées (84 Ave des Champs-Élysées) is open 24/7/365. Another 24/7 option is Pharmacie de la Porte Maillot (68 Ave de la Grande Armée).

Are online cross-border prescriptions valid in France?

Under EU cross-border healthcare directives, prescriptions issued by licensed EU physicians containing required patient and clinician details are recognized across EU member state pharmacies.

When should I call SAMU (15) or go to an emergency room (Urgences) in Paris?

For acute emergencies, dial 15 (SAMU) or European emergency 112, or visit hospital emergency departments such as Hôpital Cochin or Hôpital Saint-Louis.
