Not every condition should be treated online

The list on this page is a guide rather than a promise that every condition can be diagnosed or treated remotely.

A clinician needs enough information to assess whether video consultation is safe and appropriate. Some symptoms can be discussed remotely, while others require physical examination, laboratory testing, imaging, procedures or immediate treatment.

For ongoing conditions, remote follow-up may sometimes complement local care. The clinician can also advise when coordination with a local doctor or specialist is needed.
