Local Healthcare in Florence

Pharmacies & Emergency Guidance for Florence

Essential medical contacts and pharmacy information for travelers and students in Florence.

💊 24-Hour Pharmacies in Florence

The primary 24/7 continuous pharmacy in Florence is Farmacia Comunale Santa Maria Novella (inside the SMN railway station). In the historic center, Farmacia Molteni (Via dei Calzaiuoli) is also available for extended hours.

🚑 Florence Public Emergency Care

For urgent emergencies, dial 112. Central emergency care is located at Ospedale Santa Maria Nuova (Piazza Santa Maria Nuova, near the Duomo). For out-of-hours non-emergency public service, contact Guardia Medica Firenze at 055 6938.

Questions about Florence

Frequently asked questions

Can study-abroad students and tourists consult an online doctor in Florence?

Yes. Students from American and international programs (NYU, Syracuse, Stanford, FUA) and tourists in Florence can consult English-speaking Italian doctors online for urgent care, advice, and prescriptions.

Where is the 24-hour pharmacy in Florence for urgent prescriptions?

Farmacia Comunale inside Santa Maria Novella (SMN) railway station is open 24/7/365. Another central option is Farmacia Molteni on Via dei Calzaiuoli.

Can an online doctor provide medical notes or fit-to-fly certificates in Florence?

Yes. Following clinical assessment, our Italian-registered physicians can provide official medical certificates, university illness notes, and travel letters.

When should I go to an emergency hospital (Pronto Soccorso) in Florence?

For severe, life-threatening symptoms, dial 112 or visit Ospedale Santa Maria Nuova (in Florence city centre) or Careggi University Hospital immediately.
