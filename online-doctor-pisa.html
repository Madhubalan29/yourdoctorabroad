Healthcare while abroad

Medical advice when your usual doctor is far away

Pisa is an essential Tuscan travel and academic hub, welcoming thousands of international university students, researchers, and global tourists arriving through Pisa International Airport (Galileo Galilei). Getting sick while away from your usual doctor can disrupt travel plans and studies.

Your Doctor Abroad connects you directly with English-speaking licensed Italian doctors online. For urgent but non-life-threatening symptoms, remote consultations provide prompt clinical evaluation, electronic prescriptions, and official sick notes without language barriers or clinic queues.

Local Healthcare in Pisa

Pharmacies & Emergency Guidance for Pisa

Practical medical resources for students, researchers, and visitors in Pisa.

💊 24-Hour Pharmacies in Pisa

For urgent medication pickup, Farmacia Comunale 1 (Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, near Pisa Centrale) provides continuous service. Additional pharmacies in central Pisa operate on rotating weekend and night duty schedules.

🚑 Pisa Public Emergency Care

For emergency assistance, dial 112. Major specialized hospital care is centered at Ospedale di Cisanello (Azienda Ospedaliero-Universitaria Pisana). For non-emergency public out-of-hours triage, call Guardia Medica Pisa at 050 959866.

⏱️ Fast Online Care from €15

Don't lose time in Tuscan emergency rooms for minor health issues. For allergies, sudden coughs, eye infections, stomach upsets, or prescription refills, consult an English-speaking doctor in minutes.

Questions about Pisa

Frequently asked questions

Can University of Pisa students and international visitors consult an online doctor?

Yes. Students from University of Pisa, Sant'Anna, and Scuola Normale, as well as tourists visiting the Piazza dei Miracoli, can consult English-speaking Italian doctors online.

Where are 24-hour pharmacies in Pisa for urgent prescription refills?

Farmacia Comunale 1 at Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II (near Pisa Centrale railway station) offers continuous night and day dispensing services.

Can an online doctor provide university sick notes and certificates in Pisa?

Yes. Following clinical assessment, our licensed Italian physicians can issue official medical certificates and illness notes valid for university classes and travel.

When should I go to an emergency hospital (Pronto Soccorso) in Pisa?

In severe medical emergencies, dial 112 or visit the emergency department at Ospedale Cisanello (AOU Pisana) immediately.
