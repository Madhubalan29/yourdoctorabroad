Healthcare while abroad

Medical advice when your usual doctor is far away

The Netherlands is a popular destination for international students, tourists, professionals and long-term residents. When you are away from your usual healthcare provider, even a common illness can raise practical questions about language, access and the right level of care.

Your Doctor Abroad provides a route to request information about online medical consultation for eligible urgent situations. Whether remote care is appropriate depends on the symptoms, the patient’s location and applicable local rules. A clinician may recommend local in-person assessment when examination or testing is necessary.

Your Doctor Abroad is designed for people who may be travelling, studying, working or living away from home. You can describe your symptoms and circumstances through the booking process, and a clinician can decide whether a remote consultation is suitable. This is different from immediate local medical service and does not replace local immediate local medical care.

Common reasons people ask for help

What an online consultation may cover

Suitability is assessed individually. Examples include everyday symptoms, medication questions and follow-up concerns that can be discussed safely by video.

Everyday symptoms

Coughs, colds, sore throat, allergies, headaches, stomach symptoms, minor skin problems and other common complaints may sometimes be assessed remotely.

Medication questions

Discuss a medication concern, a treatment question or a follow-up issue. Any prescribing decision depends on clinical assessment and applicable local rules.

Follow-up care

If you already have a diagnosis or treatment plan, a clinician may be able to discuss progress and next steps when remote review is clinically appropriate.

How it works

Request a consultation in a few steps

1. Tell us where you are

Provide your current location, preferred language and a short description of what you need help with.

2. Continue on WhatsApp

Your request can open WhatsApp with the information ready to send, so you do not need to install a separate patient app.

3. Speak with a clinician

We confirm the available consultation route and timing. The clinician decides whether remote care is appropriate.

Before you book

Important limits of online care

Not every health problem can be diagnosed or treated online. A clinician may recommend a local clinic, pharmacy, diagnostic service or hospital or local clinic if examination, testing or immediate treatment is needed. Prescription decisions are made by the clinician and are subject to the law and professional requirements that apply to the patient and clinician.

Questions about the Netherlands

Frequently asked questions

Can expats and visitors in the Netherlands request online care?

Eligible urgent patients can request information about an online consultation. The clinician will determine whether remote care is appropriate.

What happens if I need an in-person assessment?

The clinician can recommend that you seek local in-person care when a physical examination, testing or other service is required.

Can an online doctor help with a common illness?

Some common illnesses and symptoms can be assessed remotely, but suitability depends on the individual clinical situation.
