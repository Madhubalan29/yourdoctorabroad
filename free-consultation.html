Eligibility and availability

How free consultation requests are reviewed

Free consultation places are intended for students and people facing genuine financial difficulty. Availability depends on the funds available for the program and on clinician availability. A request therefore does not guarantee that a free appointment will be available.

When you request help, provide enough information for the team to understand your circumstances. Students may be asked to show valid student identification. People requesting assistance because of financial hardship may be asked for reasonable supporting information.

The same clinical standards apply to free consultations as to paid consultations. A clinician will decide whether the problem is suitable for remote care and may recommend local in-person assessment when necessary.

The clinician will assess whether the concern can be managed through an online consultation and will advise if an in-person medical assessment is needed.
