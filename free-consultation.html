Free Healthcare Initiative

Free Online Doctor Consultation for Students & Financial Hardship

Healthcare is a fundamental human right. If you are sick away from home and cannot afford medical fees, Your Doctor Abroad provides 100% free online consultations with licensed English-speaking physicians through our subsidized donation fund.

🎓 International & Erasmus Students

Studying abroad on a tight budget? Show a valid university student ID or Erasmus Student Network (ESN) card to receive free doctor consultations, medical notes, and prescriptions.

🤝 Financial Hardship & Low Income

Facing genuine financial distress, unemployment, or an unexpected medical emergency while travelling? We believe cost should never prevent you from seeing a doctor.

🩺 100% Full-Service Medical Care

Free consultations receive the exact same clinical care as paid appointments: 1-on-1 video assessment with Italian-registered doctors, e-prescriptions, and 1 week of free follow-up support.

Program FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions About Free Consultations

Everything you need to know about eligibility, doctor credentials, and availability.

Who qualifies for a free online doctor consultation?

Free consultations are available for international students, Erasmus participants (ESN card holders), and travelers or expats facing genuine financial difficulty, unemployment, or hardship.

What medical conditions can be treated in a free consultation?

Our doctors treat everyday non-emergency travel ailments such as fever, coughs, colds, flu, sore throats, allergies, urinary tract infections (UTIs), stomach upsets, rashes, and medication consultations.

Are electronic prescriptions and medical notes included for free?

Yes. When clinically appropriate, our licensed Italian physician will issue an official EU electronic prescription or university sick note at no additional charge.

How is this free healthcare initiative funded?

Free consultations are funded through donations and subsidized by our team to support vulnerable travelers and students. If you'd like to support our fund, you can learn more here.

Can afford to pay standard fees?

Standard consultations start from just €15 and help us continue providing free care to students in need.
