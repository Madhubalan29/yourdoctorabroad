For physicians

What we look for in doctors joining the network

Your Doctor Abroad is developing a network intended for patients who need understandable medical care while travelling or living abroad. We are interested in licensed physicians who value clear communication, professional standards and safe use of telemedicine.

Professional approach

Doctors should work within the scope of their licence, follow applicable telemedicine rules and recognise when a patient needs an in-person examination or immediate local medical service. Experience communicating with international patients is useful because travellers may be unfamiliar with the local healthcare system.

What to include when applying

Applications should provide relevant professional qualifications, current registration or licence information, clinical interests, languages spoken and experience with remote consultations. We may request additional information needed to verify professional eligibility before a doctor is added to the network.

Joining the network does not mean that every doctor is available in every country. Consultation availability depends on clinician location, licensing, scheduling and the rules applicable to the patient and clinician.
