Local Healthcare in Milan

Pharmacies & Emergency Guidance for Milan

Essential healthcare resources for tourists, corporate travelers, students, and expats in Milan.

💊 24-Hour Pharmacies in Milan

Key continuous-service pharmacies include Farmacia Stazione Centrale (inside Milano Centrale, Piazza Duca d'Aosta) and Farmacia Carlo Erba (Piazza Duomo 19). They dispense urgent prescription medications 24 hours a day.

🚑 Milan Public Emergency Care

For acute emergencies, dial 112. Major public hospitals with emergency departments include Ospedale Niguarda and Policlinico di Milano. For non-emergency public out-of-hours triage in Lombardy, dial 116 117 (*Continuità Assistenziale*).

Questions about Milan

Frequently asked questions

Can I consult an English-speaking online doctor in Milan?

Yes. Your Doctor Abroad connects you with licensed Italian physicians fluent in English for fast online consultations, electronic prescriptions, and medical advice across Milan.

Where are 24-hour pharmacies located in Milan for emergency prescriptions?

Key 24/7 pharmacies in Milan include Farmacia Stazione Centrale (inside Milano Centrale) and Farmacia Carlo Erba at Piazza Duomo 19. Both dispense prescription medications around the clock.

What should I do if I need urgent non-emergency medical care at night in Milan?

For non-life-threatening after-hours care, you can book an online consultation with our doctor or call Lombardy's Guardia Medica service by dialing 116 117.

When should I go to an emergency room (Pronto Soccorso) in Milan?

In severe emergencies (such as chest pain, severe trauma, or acute breathing difficulty), call 112 or visit an emergency hospital such as Ospedale Niguarda or Policlinico di Milano immediately.
