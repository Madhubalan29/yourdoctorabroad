Healthcare while abroad

Medical advice when your usual doctor is far away

Naples is the vibrant gateway to southern Italy, Campania, and the Amalfi Coast. When travel illnesses, food-related stomach bugs, allergies, or sudden infections occur away from home, language barriers and unfamiliar procedures can make getting prompt medical care overwhelming.

Your Doctor Abroad gives travelers, students at University of Naples Federico II, and international residents direct access to English-speaking licensed doctors online. Speak with a doctor via video or messaging to receive rapid clinical advice, e-prescriptions, and proper medical triage.

Local Healthcare in Naples

Pharmacies & Emergency Guidance for Naples

Practical medical resources for tourists, coastal transit travelers, and expats in Naples.

💊 24-Hour Pharmacies in Naples

For night and weekend prescription dispensing, Farmacia Centrale Garibaldi (Piazza Garibaldi, right at Napoli Centrale) provides 24/7 service. Along Corso Umberto I, several pharmacies operate on night duty rotas.

🚑 Naples Public Emergency Care

For life-threatening situations, call 112 immediately. The regional general hospital is Ospedale Antonio Cardarelli. For non-emergency public out-of-hours triage, reach Guardia Medica Napoli at 081 751 3177.

⏱️ Fast Online Care from €15

Public emergency clinics in Naples can have extensive wait times for routine conditions. For gastrointestinal upsets, earaches, fever, or lost medications, consult an English-speaking doctor in minutes.

Questions about Naples

Frequently asked questions

Can tourists visiting Naples and the Amalfi Coast consult an online doctor?

Yes. Whether in central Naples, Pompeii, Sorrento, or along the Amalfi Coast, travelers can access fast English-speaking online medical care for travel ailments and advice.

Where are 24-hour pharmacies located in Naples for emergency medications?

Farmacia Centrale Garibaldi at Piazza Garibaldi (near Napoli Centrale) provides 24/7 continuous pharmacy dispensing. Look for the green pharmacy cross.

How do I get an electronic prescription in Naples?

Following an online clinical assessment with our Italian-registered physician, an electronic prescription (NRE / barcode) is issued directly to your phone and accepted at all Italian pharmacies.

When should I go to an emergency hospital (Pronto Soccorso) in Naples?

For severe trauma, acute chest pain, or serious emergencies, call 112 immediately or proceed to major hospital emergency departments such as Ospedale Cardarelli.
