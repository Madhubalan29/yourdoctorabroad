Support Your Doctor Abroad

Our mission

Healthcare shouldn't depend on your financial conditions.

Your Doctor Abroad was built to connect travellers, expats and locals with hassle-free, convenient urgent care, wherever they are. But we want to go further — our long-term goal is to make our medical consultations completely free, starting with the people who need them most.

We've already kept our prices as low as we can, so care stays within reach for as many people as possible while providing the best possible care available to each of our patients. Even so, we know that even a low cost can still be out of reach for some — low-income families, and people in regions with little access to a doctor at all.

Our ultimate goal goes further: make consultations completely free, at least for those who truly can't afford them, through the generosity of donors. Every donation goes directly toward covering the cost of a real consultation for someone who couldn't otherwise afford one.

Why support matters

Help make medical advice more accessible to people abroad

Your Doctor Abroad aims to make it easier for travellers, students and people living overseas to understand where to start when they have a urgent health concern. Support helps us maintain the service and can contribute to access initiatives for people who may struggle to afford a consultation.

How support can help

Contributions can help fund free or reduced-cost consultation opportunities when the relevant eligibility and available funding allow it. They can also support the practical work involved in maintaining a secure, accessible service for international patients.
