Before you submit

What information helps the doctor prepare

When requesting an appointment, give your current location, preferred language and a short description of the medical concern. If relevant, include your age, medicines, allergies, previous diagnoses and how long the symptoms have been present. Avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive information in public or unsecured channels.

Your request opens WhatsApp so the consultation team can review the information and confirm an appropriate route. The fact that a request can be submitted does not mean that every problem can be managed remotely. A clinician may recommend local examination, testing or urgent care.

Online versus in-person care

Video consultation can be convenient when a problem can reasonably be assessed remotely. If a physical examination or procedure is needed, an in-person appointment may be more appropriate. Severe symptoms should be directed to appropriate local medical care instead of this booking form.
